Our Story

Founded in 2020, Brew & Bean began with a simple mission: to serve exceptional coffee that brings people together. We source our beans directly from sustainable farms across three continents.

Our skilled baristas craft each cup with precision and care, ensuring every sip delivers the perfect balance of flavor and aroma. Whether you're here for a quick espresso or a leisurely afternoon, we've created a space where community thrives.
